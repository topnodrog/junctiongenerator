**Uniswap** is a [decentralized](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Decentralization "Decentralization") [cryptocurrency exchange](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cryptocurrency_exchange "Cryptocurrency exchange") protocol built on the [Ethereum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ethereum "Ethereum") [blockchain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blockchain "Blockchain"). It enables the automated trading of digital assets through [smart contracts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Smart_contract "Smart contract"), eliminating the need for intermediaries or centralized order books. Uniswap facilitates [cryptocurrency tokens](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cryptocurrency "Cryptocurrency") swaps using liquidity pools contributed by users.
The protocol, which runs on [open-source software](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Open-source_software "Open-source software"), was launched in 2018. Uniswap was created on November 2, 2018 by Hayden Adams, a former mechanical engineer at [Siemens](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Siemens "Siemens"). Adams is the [founder](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Founder_CEO "Founder CEO") and [CEO](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chief_executive_officer "Chief executive officer") of Uniswap Labs, the company responsible for the development of the **Uniswap Protocol** , a decentralized [exchange](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cryptocurrency_exchange "Cryptocurrency exchange") (DEX) built on the [Ethereum blockchain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ethereum "Ethereum"). Adams launched Uniswap in November 2018, after working as a [mechanical engineer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mechanical_engineering "Mechanical engineering") and being inspired by a [blog](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blog "Blog") post written by [Vitalik Buterin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vitalik_Buterin "Vitalik Buterin"), the co-founder of [Ethereum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ethereum "Ethereum"). His goal was to create a decentralized platform for exchanging [tokens](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cryptocurrency "Cryptocurrency") without relying on [centralized](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Centralisation "Centralisation") exchanges.
Uniswap operates using open-source software on [blockchain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blockchain "Blockchain") networks, and allows users to exchange cryptocurrencies directly from their wallets. Governance of the protocol is managed by holders of its native governance token, UNI, which was introduced in 2020 and distributed retroactively to early users. While UNI is used to vote on protocol upgrades and changes, it is not required to use the platform. Uniswap introduced the Automated [Market Maker](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Market_maker "Market maker") (AMM) model, which allows users to trade [cryptocurrencies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cryptocurrency "Cryptocurrency") directly from their [wallets](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cryptocurrency_wallet "Cryptocurrency wallet").
### Uniswap Labs
Uniswap Labs is a software company founded in 2018 by [Hayden Adams](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hayden_Adams "Hayden Adams"). The company's early investors included venture capital firms like [Andreessen Horowitz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andreessen_Horowitz "Andreessen Horowitz"), [Paradigm Operations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paradigm_Operations "Paradigm Operations"), [Union Square Ventures](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Union_Square_Ventures "Union Square Ventures") LLC and ParaFi.
In 2022 Uniswap Labs had raised $165 million, after announcing in 2021 that it was looking to raise between $100 and $200 million and was working with Polychain.
Uniswap offers products such as a self-custodial mobile [wallet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cryptocurrency_wallet "Cryptocurrency wallet"), a trading [API](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/API "API") for professional users, and a [web interface](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/User_interface "User interface"). These tools provide access to liquidity across multiple [blockchains](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blockchain "Blockchain"), including [Ethereum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ethereum "Ethereum"), [Polygon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polygon_\(blockchain\) "Polygon \(blockchain\)"), and [Optimism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Optimism_\(blockchain\) "Optimism \(blockchain\)").[]
### Uniswap Foundation
The Uniswap Foundation was founded in 2022 by Devin Walsh and Ken Ng, after 95% of Uniswap token holders voted in favor of the proposal. The Foundation facilitates community governance of the Uniswap DAO, development of the Uniswap Protocol, and communication among contributors. Uniswap Foundation's most recent funding proposal, Uniswap Unleashed, outlined a plan to make the Uniswap Protocol the world’s infrastructure for digital value transfer with significant support for Uniswap v4 and Unichain. It passed with overwhelming support.

## Protocol
[Constant product formula](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constant_function_market_maker "Constant function market maker")
Uniswap acts as an automated [market maker](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Market_maker "Market maker") and uses liquidity pools to fulfill orders, instead of relying on a traditional market maker, with an aim to create more efficient markets. Individuals and bots—termed "[liquidity providers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Market_maker "Market maker")"—provide liquidity to the exchange by adding a pair of tokens to a smart contract which can be bought and sold by other users according to the [constant-product rule](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constant_function_market_maker "Constant function market maker") ϕ ( x , y ) = x y {\displaystyle \phi (x,y)=xy} . In return, liquidity providers are given a percentage of the trading [fees](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fee "Fee") earned for that trading pair. For each trade, a certain amount of tokens is removed from the pool for an amount of the other token, thereby changing the price. No fees are required to list tokens.
In December 2025, Uniswap governance approved the “UNIfication” proposal, enabling protocol fees (the “fee switch”) and introducing a deflationary mechanism tied to protocol revenue. Under the rollout described by Uniswap, Uniswap v2 pools would shift from a 0.30% liquidity-provider fee to 0.25% for liquidity providers plus a 0.05% protocol fee, while selected Uniswap v3 pools would route a portion of LP fees to the protocol depending on the pool’s fee tier; the proposal also included a one-time burn of 100 million UNI from the treasury after a timelock period and an ongoing burn mechanism funded by protocol fees (including sequencer revenue from Unichain after costs and allocations).
## UNI token
UNI is the [governance token](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Governance_token?action=edit&redlink=1 "Governance token \(page does not exist\)") of the Uniswap protocol, enabling holders to participate in the platform's decentralized [governance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Governance "Governance").
The token allows holders to propose and vote on protocol upgrades, changes to [fee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fee "Fee") structures, and other governance-related matters. UNI operates on the Ethereum blockchain following the ERC-20 standard, ensuring compatibility with a wide range of [decentralized applications](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Decentralized_application "Decentralized application"). Its total supply is capped, with a scheduled release over several years to support ongoing development and ecosystem growth.
