## Phase 2: Email Strategy (Wed)

### Cold Email Formula (for investors without warm intro)

**Subject Line:** [Problem or Proof]
- "Crypto fraud losses hit $14.4B (and we're solving it)"
- "Layer-1 junctioning + deterministic verification"
- "We shipped a testnet. You should see it."

**Body Structure:**
1. **Hook** (1 sentence) — Problem or shipped fact
2. **Why now** (2-3 sentences) — Market moment, urgency
3. **What we built** (2-3 sentences) — Ship, live proof, concrete milestone
4. **Why we're reaching out** (1 sentence) — Thesis alignment
5. **Clear ask** (1 sentence) — 15-20 min call, specific dates

**Word count:** 100-150 words max (no wall of text)

**Tone:** Matter-of-fact, not salesy. You're informing them of something interesting they probably care about.

## What NOT to Do (Cold Email Killers)

❌ "We're disrupting the space"  
❌ "Revolutionary AI verification"  
❌ Long company story or vision  
❌ Generic template (obvious you sent 100)  
❌ Attachments (use links instead)  
❌ Ask for $1M in first email  
❌ Multiple recipients in To: (use BCC or send individually)

## What to Do if You Get a Call

**First 15 minutes:**
1. Thank them for time (genuine)
2. Ask what they know about the problem (listen more than talk)
3. Explain what you shipped + why it matters
4. Share one use case or metric
5. Ask what they're thinking (don't push)

**Close:**
- "Would it make sense to talk next week about potentially exploring this together?"
- If yes → schedule follow-up
- If no → "Thanks for the time. I'll send you updates as we progress. Would that be useful?"

**Tone:** You're informing them of an opportunity they might care about, not asking permission.
