## Subject Line Strategies

**What Gets Opened:**
- Specific problem + proof ("Costly verification gap — local audit layer shipped")
- Relevant portfolio reference ("You backed Eigen, here's the verification flip-side")
- Technical intrigue ("Deterministic replay + collusion-resistant quorum")

**What Gets Deleted:**
- "Disruptive AI/Web3/Blockchain" (generic)
- "Exciting opportunity" (salesy)
- "Quick favor?" (spammy)
- "We should talk" (why would they?)

**Best performers (for crypto):**
- Lead with a metric only when it is current, scoped, and cited
- Lead with shipped proof ("Local testnet validated; audit consensus shipped")
- Lead with relevant problem ("Validator collusion prevention")

## Pro Tips

**Send from your own email** (not a generic contact form)
- Personalizes the email
- Ensures replies come back to you
- Looks more legitimate

**Send one-to-one, not mass CC**
- Use BCC if sending batch
- Each email feels personal

**Best time to send:** 
- Tuesday-Thursday, 9am-11am their timezone
- Avoid Monday (busy) and Friday (not reading)

**Optimal frequency:**
- Max 3 emails per day (sending too many looks spammy)
- Space them out: morning batch, afternoon batch

**Include a clear next step:**
- "Are you around Thursday for a quick call?"
- Specific dates/times if possible
- Use Calendly link if you want (but optional)

**Don't:** Attach PDFs or decks
- Use links: "Here's the deck: [junctiongenerator.net/pitch]"
- Attachments = immediately trashed
