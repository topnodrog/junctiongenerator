**For your cold outreach:** This transforms "spray and pray" into "data-driven follow-up"

## Using Visible for Cold Outreach

### Workflow:
1. **Find investor on Crunchbase**
2. **Add to Visible CRM** (company, email, notes)
3. **Send email with deck link** — Use Visible's link so you track opens
4. **Wait 3 days**
5. **Check Visible dashboard** — Did they open? Which slides?
6. **Follow up based on engagement** (see follow-up strategy below)

### Email Example (with Visible link):

```
Hi [Name],

We shipped a Layer-1 verification network. Live testnet, junctioning Layer-1 live, 
full verification model working.

Here's the deck: [VISIBLE_LINK]

Worth 15 min to discuss?

—James
```

When they click the link, Visible tracks:
- Did they open it?
- How long did they spend on each slide?
- Which slide got most attention?
- Did they come back?

## Follow-up Strategy Based on Visible Engagement

### Scenario 1: They Opened Deck + Spent 5+ mins

**Their behavior:** Interested enough to look

**Your follow-up (Day 3):**
```
Hi [Name],

Saw you checked out the deck. A few thoughts:

- Slide 5 (Technical Architecture) is the core — deterministic replay + multi-challenger 
  quorum is how we avoid the oracle problem.
- Slide 8 (Market Opportunity) breaks down the $14.4B TAM.

Any questions on the approach? Would love to walk through it.

—James
```

### Scenario 2: They Opened But Only Looked at One Slide

**Their behavior:** Glanced at problem / high-level, didn't dig into details

**Your follow-up (Day 4):**
```
Hi [Name],

One question based on your interest: Are you more focused on the 
[consensus/market/economic security] angle? Happy to dive deep on whichever 
resonates most.

—James
```

Tailor next conversation to what they actually cared about.

### Scenario 3: They Didn't Open at All

**Their behavior:** Ignored your email

**Your follow-up (Day 3):**
```
Hi [Name],

Quick follow-up in case that got buried. One-pager below if you prefer to skim:

Problem: $14.4B crypto fraud losses
Solution: Deterministic verification + multi-challenger quorum
Proof: Testnet live, junctioning Layer-1 shipped

Interested in a brief chat?

—James
```

If still no response by Day 7, move on to next investor.

### Scenario 4: They Opened, Looked at Everything, Came Back Multiple Times

**Their behavior:** Seriously interested

**Your action:**
- Don't wait for Day 3 follow-up
- Email same day: "Noticed you reviewed the deck thoroughly — impressive! 
  Available for a call Thursday?"
- This person is a priority

## Visible vs. Spreadsheet

**Use Visible if:**
- You want automatic engagement tracking
- You're sending deck links (shows per-slide engagement)
- You want professional appearance (data rooms, updates)
- Budget allows ($129/month for Core plan)

**Use Spreadsheet if:**
- You're just sending emails (no deck links)
- You want simplicity
- You're broke (free)

**Hybrid approach (recommended):**
- Visible: Manage deck sharing + engagement tracking
- Spreadsheet: Track emails, calls, status

Template:
```
Name | Email | Date Sent | Subject | Opened? | Deck Viewed? | Reply? | Call Scheduled? | Status | Notes
```

## Best Practices

### Email Frequency
- **Day 1:** Initial send
- **Day 3:** Light follow-up ("Did this land?")
- **Day 7:** Final attempt ("One more time")
- **Day 10+:** Mark as "pass" unless they reply

### Personalization Level
- **High effort:** Custom email + specific mention of their portfolio
- **Medium effort:** Template with investor name + one detail
- **Low effort:** Pure template (don't do this for cold — conversion %)

### Response Time
- **Your target:** Reply to investor within 4 hours (shows you're serious)
- **If they ask for call:** Respond same day with Calendly link

### Deck Updates
- **Update deck every 2 weeks** — New metrics, progress updates
- **Re-share with Tier 1 investors** — "We've shipped X since we last connected"
- **Use new data as hook** — "Just hit 50 validators on testnet — worth a look?"

## Troubleshooting Visible

| Problem | Solution |
|---------|----------|
| Deck link not working | Regenerate link in Visible |
| Can't see engagement stats | Make sure you uploaded PDF version, not just pptx |
| Investor didn't get email | Check spam folder, resend |
| Need to update deck | Upload new version, share new link |
| Want to track emails sent from Gmail | Use Visible's email integration |

## What To Do With Engagement Data

### High Engagement (3+ visits, 5+ min total)
→ Call this person this week. They're interested.

### Medium Engagement (1-2 visits, 2-3 min)
→ Follow-up Friday asking specific question about what interested them.

### Low Engagement (Opened once, <1 min)
→ Send follow-up with one-pager instead. Maybe deck isn't the issue.

### No Engagement (Never opened)
→ Change subject line or send from different angle next time.
