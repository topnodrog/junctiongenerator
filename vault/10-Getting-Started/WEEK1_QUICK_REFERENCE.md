## Email Quick Formula

**Subject:** [Problem or Proof]  
**Example:** "Crypto verification failures remain costly — we shipped a local audit layer"

**Body Structure:**
1. Hook: One sentence problem
2. What we built: 2-3 sentences + proof
3. Why you: 1 sentence thesis alignment
4. Call to action: "15-20 min call Thursday?"

**Tone:** Informational, not salesy

**Length:** 100-150 words max

## Red Flags to Avoid

❌ "Revolutionary" / "Disruptive"  
❌ Attachments (use links)  
❌ Long email (>150 words)  
❌ Generic greeting ("Dear Sir/Madam")  
❌ Mass CC (use BCC)  
❌ No clear next step

## Emergency: If You're Getting Opens But No Replies

**Likely causes:**
1. Deck isn't compelling (they read it, aren't interested)
2. Call-to-action isn't clear (add "15-min call Thursday?")
3. They're busy (follow-up more aggressively)

**Fixes:**
- Change subject line for next batch
- Make deck CTA clearer
- Follow-up faster (24 hours, not 3 days)
