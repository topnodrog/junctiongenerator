## Logo Variations

### 1. Full Logo with Text (Primary)
- **Use for:** Presentations, investor materials, brand headers
- **Minimum size:** 200px width
- **File formats:** SVG (scalable), PNG (transparent background)

### 2. Icon-Only (Nexus Symbol)
- **Use for:** Favicon, app icon, social media profile pictures
- **Sizes:** 32px, 64px, 128px, 256px, 512px
- **Best for:** Quick brand recognition, compact spaces

### 3. Horizontal Lockup
- **Use for:** Website headers, email signatures, document footers
- **Ratio:** Wide (4:1 or 5:1)
- **Best for:** Horizontal spaces, navigation bars

### 4. Monochrome (Emerald)
- **Use for:** Embossing, single-color printing, conservative applications
- **Color:** `#1FD9A6` on white or dark backgrounds
- **Best for:** Professional documents, certifications, legal materials

### 5. Inverted (Light Background)
- **Use for:** Light backgrounds, white/cream websites
- **Adjustment:** Darker outlines and nodes for contrast
- **Best for:** Web designs, light-themed applications

## Clear Space & Sizing

**Minimum clear space:** 20px around all edges
**Minimum width:** 200px (larger is always better)
**Do not scale below:** 32px (icon becomes illegible)

## Do's & Don'ts

✅ **DO:**
- Use on dark backgrounds (premium feel)
- Scale proportionally
- Maintain 20px clear space
- Use SVG for web (crisp at any size)
- Keep the dual-color scheme for brand recognition

❌ **DON'T:**
- Rotate or skew the logo
- Change the colors without approval
- Remove the text for extended use
- Use below 32px without icon version
- Place on busy/patterned backgrounds
